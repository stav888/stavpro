# The Whispering Library

**Series:** YTKIDS1
**Format:** 8-panel comic page (2 columns x 4 rows)
**Characters:** the girl with the blue hair, her friend the boy in the yellow t-shirt, her friend the girl with curly black hair in the green dress, a white rabbit, and a small bird

---

**1. A Mysterious Book**
The three friends visit a quiet library. Suddenly an old book starts to glow and whisper:
**Book:** "There is a story waiting for your help!"

**2. The Painted Door**
The book opens, and a colorful painted forest rises from its pages. A small door appears on the page and opens into the painted world.
**Girl:** "Wow! Can we go into the story?"

**3. The Story Gets Stuck**
The friends meet a painted rabbit who is looking for his friend, a small bird. The story stops before he finds her.
**Rabbit:** "I don't know how to finish the story..."

**4. Thinking Together**
The friends share ideas: maybe the bird is hiding behind a giant flower, or maybe she left colorful feathers behind.
**Boy:** "Let's look for clues together!"

**5. The Path of Colors**
They notice small colored dots on the path and follow them at a calm pace, staying together.
**Girl:** "Here's another dot! We're on the right track."

**6. Friends Come Together**
Behind a flower bush they find the bird. She smiles and flies to the rabbit.
**Bird:** "I was looking for you!"
**Rabbit:** "How wonderful that we found each other!"

**7. A New Ending**
The characters paint a happy picture together. The final words appear on the page:
**Narrator:** "And so, thanks to friendship and helping each other, the story had a happy ending."

**8. Back to the Library**
The friends return through the painted door. The book closes gently, and its cover reads "To be continued...".
**Boy:** "What a great story!"
**Narrator:** "In that library, there is always another story waiting for anyone who is ready to imagine."

**The End... for now.**
