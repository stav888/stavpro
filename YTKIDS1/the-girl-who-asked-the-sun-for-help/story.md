# The Girl Who Asked the Sun for Help

**Series:** YTKIDS1
**Format:** 8-panel comic, 9:16 vertical

---

One morning, the city woke up slowly. The sun was tired, and its light was weak and yellowish. The rooftops looked grey, and the flowers in the garden bowed their heads.

Every day, the sun sends its light up to the great mirror on the lighthouse, and the mirror bounces it down over the whole city. But lately the mirror has been covered in dust, so the light gets stuck and the sun has to push harder and harder to reach the town.

The girl with the blue hair stepped into the street, her red backpack on her back and the golden star box under her arm. She looked up and saw the sun drooping above the lighthouse.

"Sun, sun, what's wrong?" she asked.

The sun sighed and answered in a faint voice: "I'm getting tired. I have to push so hard to send my light down to the city, and the dust on the mirror keeps it from going through."

The girl thought for a moment, then smiled. "Then let's make it easier for you. I'll climb to the lighthouse and see what's wrong with the mirror."

She climbed the stone steps, one step at a time, until she reached the top. There she found a huge round mirror, covered in dust.

"The mirror is dirty!" she said.

She opened the golden star box, and the stars inside began to glow. She gathered soft leaves and gently wiped the mirror, and the light began to flow again, warm and strong.

The clean mirror caught the sunlight and sent it down to the city with ease. The sun felt its strength return and said happily, "Thank you! Now my light can reach everyone without me getting tired."

The girl laughed. "And now the whole city can have good dreams tonight."

That evening, as the sun set gently, the city was full of soft, happy light. The girl watched the first stars appear and promised herself she would come back to help again whenever she was needed.

**The End**
