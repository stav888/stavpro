## Script / narration
Final English script:

### `script_en.txt`

Source: `history-storyteller/examples/flexible-glass/script_en.txt`

```
Almost two thousand years ago, a glassmaker came to the palace of the Roman emperor Tiberius. He brought a gift: a beautiful, crystal-clear glass bowl. In front of the emperor, he threw it hard onto the floor.

The bowl did not shatter. It only dented, like metal. The glassmaker took out a small hammer, and tapped it back into shape. Tiberius asked him: does anyone else know this secret? Proud, the glassmaker swore that no one else did.

But Tiberius feared this glass would make gold and silver worthless. He ordered the glassmaker executed, and the secret died with him. That's the story told by the Roman writers Pliny and Petronius. To this day, no one knows if this glass ever really existed.
```
