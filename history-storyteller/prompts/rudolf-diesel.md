On-screen title: THE MAN WHO INVENTED DIESEL AND VANISHED

Narration (10 parts):

1. In 1913, the inventor of the diesel engine vanished from a ship in the middle of the night. What happened that night is still a mystery.
2. Twenty years earlier, Rudolf Diesel received a patent for a new kind of engine.
3. A year later, he improved the design. The diesel engine was born.
4. It made him famous. Engines bearing his name began to power factories and ships around the world.
5. But by 1913, Diesel was in serious financial trouble.
6. On the night of 29 September, he boarded the SS Dresden from Antwerp to Harwich, on his way to London.
7. After dinner, he went to his cabin, and asked to be woken at 6:15.
8. In the morning, he was gone. His bed had not been slept in. His hat and neatly folded overcoat were found by the ship's railing.
9. About ten days later, Dutch sailors found a body in the sea. It was too decomposed to keep. His son, Eugen, identified the personal items they recovered.
10. How he died remains unexplained. The inventor of the diesel engine vanished in the night, and the mystery was never solved.
