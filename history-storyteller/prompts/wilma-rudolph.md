### `sentences.txt`

Source: `history-storyteller/examples/wilma-rudolph/sentences.txt`

```
In 1940, in a small Tennessee town, Wilma Rudolph was born, the twentieth of twenty-two children in a poor family.
At four, she caught polio, her left leg grew weak, and doctors said she would never walk again.
Her mother refused to give up, taking her by bus every week to a hospital in Nashville, while her brothers and sisters rubbed her leg every day.
For years she wore a metal brace, until at twelve, she took it off and played basketball.
She began to run, and at just sixteen, she won an Olympic bronze medal in the relay.
In 1960, in Rome, she won three gold medals, and the world called her the fastest woman on Earth.
Back home, she insisted her welcome parade be open to everyone, and it became her town's first integrated event.
```
