### `sentences.txt`

Source: `history-storyteller/examples/yamaguchi/sentences.txt`

```
Tsutomu Yamaguchi was a Japanese engineer.
On August 6, 1945, he was on a business trip in Hiroshima, when the first atomic bomb fell.
Burned and injured, he survived the night.
The next morning, bandaged, he took a train home — to Nagasaki.
On August 9, at work, he was telling his boss about the horror he had seen in Hiroshima.
At that moment, the second atomic bomb exploded over Nagasaki.
Incredibly, Yamaguchi survived again.
He lived to the age of 93 — the only person officially recognized to have survived both atomic bombings.
```
