## Script / narration
No separate `script_en.txt` is in the examples folder. The saved narration units are `sentences.txt`:

### `sentences.txt`

Source: `history-storyteller/examples/wojtek/sentences.txt`

```
In Iran in 1942, Polish soldiers found an orphaned brown bear cub.
They adopted him, named him Wojtek, and raised him in their unit.
He ate, slept, and marched with the men, part of the company.
To sail with them to Italy, the army officially enlisted him as a soldier.
Private Wojtek got a service number, a pay book, and later became corporal.
At Monte Cassino in 1944, soldiers said he carried crates of artillery shells.
After the war, he lived at Edinburgh Zoo, visited by old comrades.
He died in 1963 — more than a mascot, he was one of them.
```
