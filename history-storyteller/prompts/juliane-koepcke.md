On-screen title: THE GIRL WHO FELL THREE KILOMETERS

Narration (10 parts):

1. She fell three kilometers, still strapped to her seat. How did one girl walk out of the jungle alive?
2. On Christmas Eve, 1971, seventeen-year-old Juliane Koepcke boarded LANSA Flight 508 in Peru, with her mother, Maria.
3. Mid-flight, lightning struck the plane. It broke apart in the sky.
4. Juliane fell about three kilometers into the Peruvian rainforest, still strapped to her row of seats.
5. She woke with a broken collarbone, and she was alone. Of ninety-two people on board, she would be the only survivor.
6. She could not find her mother. So she began to walk, following a stream, as her parents had taught her.
7. Day after day, she waded and walked along the water, hungry, hurt, and almost too weak to stand.
8. Her wounds became infested. In a hut by the river, she treated them with gasoline.
9. The next day, local loggers found her, and took her downriver to safety.
10. Her mother did not survive. After eleven days, Juliane had fallen three kilometers, and walked out of the jungle alive.
