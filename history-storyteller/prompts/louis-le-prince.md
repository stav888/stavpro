On-screen title: THE MAN WHO INVENTED FILM AND VANISHED

Narration (10 parts):

1. In 1888, in a garden in Leeds, Louis Le Prince pointed a strange new camera at his family, and started to film.
2. The short scene he captured that day is now known as the Roundhay Garden Scene, the oldest surviving motion picture.
3. Louis Le Prince had invented an early motion-picture camera, years before the world would credit anyone else.
4. He also filmed people walking across Leeds Bridge, and prepared to show his invention in public.
5. On 16 September 1890, he boarded a train from Dijon to Paris, and never arrived.
6. No body was found. No luggage was found. Louis Le Prince had simply vanished.
7. His disappearance remains unsolved. No one has ever proven what happened on that train.
8. In the years that followed, Thomas Edison was credited as the father of motion pictures.
9. His son, Adolphe, later testified in a patent case, trying to prove his father's place in the story.
10. In 1901, Adolphe was found shot dead. The mystery of his father was never solved.
