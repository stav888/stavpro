## Fact notes
- 2560 BC: conventional date (c. 2600–2560 BC). ~146.6 m original height; ~2.3 million blocks is the standard estimate.
- Tallest for ~3,800 years (until Lincoln Cathedral, c. 1311) — script says "almost four thousand".
- Paid-workforce view is from workers' cemetery excavations (Zahi Hawass, Mark Lehner); mainstream consensus, though labour likely included corvée rotation — "paid" is accurate, "not slaves" is consensus.
- Cleopatra (d. 30 BC) vs Moon landing 1969: ~2,000 vs ~2,500 years — correct.
