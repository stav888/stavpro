1|Twenty thousand people once lived... eighty-five meters underground.
2|Beneath the soft volcanic rock of Cappadocia, a city waited in silence.
3|Its name is Derinkuyu. The deepest underground city ever found in Turkey.
4|Stables near the surface. Kitchens, storage rooms, chapels deeper below.
5|Even a school, carved into stone, far from the light of day.
6|When danger came, massive stone doors rolled shut from the inside.
7|Each floor could be sealed. Each tunnel closed like a vault.
8|Ventilation shafts kept the air alive, all the way to the surface.
9|For centuries, families hid here with their animals and their food.
10|Then the city was forgotten. Its entrances vanished under ordinary homes.
11|Until 1963, when a man knocked through his basement wall... and found a room.
12|Behind it: tunnels. Then floors. Then an entire hidden world.
13|Who dug this far into the earth, and how many more cities still sleep below?
14|Somewhere in Cappadocia, another door may still wait behind a basement wall.
