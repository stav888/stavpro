Imagine waking to the sound of feet pounding the cobblestones… and no music at all. 💃
In July 1518, in Strasbourg, a woman named Frau Troffea stepped outside her door and began to dance. She didn't stop that day, or the next, or the one after that.
One by one, her neighbors joined her. Soon there were dozens, swaying under the burning sun, their feet torn and bleeding.
The city's doctors blamed "hot blood" and prescribed more dancing. The town hired musicians and built a stage… and the dancing only spread. Finally, the dancers were carried to a mountain shrine to pray.
Poisoned bread? A city driven mad by hunger and fear? 500 years later, it's still a mystery… 🎶🩰🕯️
