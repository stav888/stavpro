1|Deep in the jungles of Mexico, giants once lay buried.
2|The Olmecs, the mother culture of Mesoamerica, vanished long ago.
3|When the first stone head was unearthed, the world was speechless.
4|Each head stood up to three meters tall, carved from solid volcanic stone.
5|The heaviest ones weighed up to forty tons.
6|And remember, the Olmecs had no wheels... and no beasts of burden.
7|To move them, hundreds of workers dragged them across miles of terrain.
8|But the stone didn't even come from nearby.
9|Some of the basalt was quarried more than fifty miles from where it now stands.
10|Log rollers, braided ropes, and rivers. Every ton a battle.
11|But why would a people without wheels move forty tons at all?
12|Some say they were rulers. Some say they were gods.
13|Like the Hanging Gardens, the heads challenge everything we think we know.
14|The Olmecs vanished, leaving only these silent giants behind.
15|Follow for more mysteries history forgot.
